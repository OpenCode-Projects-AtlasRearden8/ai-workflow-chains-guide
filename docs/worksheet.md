# The Chain Worksheet

*Print this, or fill it in on screen. Taking a job from "ugh" to steps.*

Use this whenever a job feels too big or too annoying to start. Fill it in
top to bottom — it walks you through everything from chapters 1–3. Allow
about 15 minutes, less on your second chain.

---

## Job, stated messily

Write the job the way it sounds in your head, no tidying:

> **The job:** ________________________________________________

## Step 0 — Name the goal (goal-first slicing)

Paste the messy job into your AI chat:

```text
"Here's my messy brain-dump of a job: '[paste your messy version]'.
In one or two sentences, what am I actually trying to do or decide?
Ask me up to 2 clarifying questions first if needed."
```

> **The goal (their answer, checked and lightly edited):**
> ________________________________________________

**CHECK:** Does this sentence, read in your own voice, name a job you'd
actually accept doing? If not, re-run once with more context from you.

## Step 1 — Slice into steps

Ask for the slices (don't trust, then do trust):

```text
"The goal is: '[your goal sentence]'. Propose a 3- or 4-step plan where
each step is ONE prompt to you, and each step's answer can be checked
by a non-expert in under 30 seconds. Show each step as:
  Step N — what I ask you — what I get back."
```

Now write the steps in the boxes below, trimming as you go:

| Step | I will ask (in a line or two) | I expect back |
|------|--------------------------------|---------------|
| 1 |                                |               |
| 2 |                                |               |
| 3 |                                |               |
| (4) |                              |               |

**The one-breath test:** say your chain out loud ("first I ask for the
buckets, then..., then..."). Too long a breath? Merge a step. Too short?
Good.

## Step 2 — Write the actual prompts

For each step, write the prompt you'll paste — with `[BLANK]`s for things
you must supply and `[PASTE FROM STEP N]` where the previous answer goes:

```text
STEP 1 ASK: "
[...write it...]
"
STEP 1 CHECK (a 30-second question): "

"
STEP 1 PASTE INTO: step ___

STEP 2 ASK: "
[...containing: [PASTE FROM STEP 1]...]
"
STEP 2 CHECK: "
"
STEP 2 PASTE INTO: step ___

STEP 3 ASK: "
[...]
"
STEP 3 CHECK: "
"
STEP 3 PASTE INTO: ✔ end of chain — this is my final answer
```

**Seam hygiene, quickly:** every paste is framed ("Here are the notes:"),
bounded ("using only these facts") and shaped ("Format: bullet list"). Three
phrases, most of the reliability.

## Step 5 — Harvest (2 minutes, do it today)

Copy the prompts you **actually used** into a file named after the chain,
add your `CHECK` lines and the watch-out notes, and save it in your chain
folder. Next time this job comes up, you start from the file.
